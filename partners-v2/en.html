02 / Partner value

Why partner with Jaree Club?

Introduce your benefits to a focused audience and build your brand’s presence within the Jaree community.

01

A focused audience

A trusted investor community, including high-net-worth individuals, alongside startups seeking services that support their growth.

02

Visibility on Jaree’s website

Your brand and approved benefits appear in Jaree Club, with offer details, redemption instructions and your links.

03

Special placement for distinctive benefits

Partners offering distinctive benefits may receive featured placement within Jaree Club, based on the benefit’s value and relevance to members.
